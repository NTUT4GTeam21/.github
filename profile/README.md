## Hi, we're Team 21 👋

We're six students from the **NTU Honours College Tech for Good** programme, working with **SADeaf** (Singapore Association for the Deaf) to support children of Deaf adults (CODAs) in Singapore.

### What we're working on

Many CODAs take on interpreting and other adult responsibilities from a young age, and some need extra support with English literacy. SADeaf runs a reading programme to help, led by rotating volunteers.

We're building the **CODA Reading Toolkit**, a web app that helps volunteers:

- 📚 find books suited to each child's reading level
- 📝 run engaging 20-minute reading sessions with ready-made word cards and discussion prompts
- 🤝 hand over smoothly to the next volunteer with session notes and history
- 🧭 handle common situations with a short, practical volunteer guide

### How we build

- **Teaching stays human.** The toolkit prepares and records; volunteers teach.
- **Privacy first.** We collect the least information about children that works.
- **Built to last.** SADeaf can maintain the content after our project ends.
